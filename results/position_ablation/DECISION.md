# Position features (GPU PC, train_size 1,024, 64,000 steps, CUDA) — decision

Tables: `SUMMARY.md` (generated by `scripts/summarize.py compare|rate|paired|matched`).
Sample: 200 validation images (development scale), one training seed per variant.

**Winner: bit_plane_rowcol** (bit + bit-plane + row/col embeddings, 171,297 parameters)
-> base for `configs/training_modes.json`.

Primary evidence: rate at acceptable quality (complete-file bpp needed for pooled PSNR >=
20/25/30/35 dB, paired image bootstrap). bit_plane_rowcol needs fewer bits than bit_only at
every target, with both payload codings; every 95% CI excludes 0:

- raw payload: -0.68 / -0.50 / -0.64 / -0.35 bpp at 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 dB;
- range-coded payload: -0.14 / -0.19 / -0.21 / -0.22 bpp.

bit_plane alone is a smaller, mostly significant gain (raw 20-30 dB, range-coded 25-35 dB).

Why the metric: the harness's operational-envelope mean averages the PSNR gain over the whole
0.27-8.27 bpp overlap. Here it disagreed with itself (raw +0.51 dB [+0.12, +0.85] for
bit_plane_rowcol, range-coded -1.60 dB [-1.97, -1.25]) because most of that range is 5-16 dB
output where no one would operate: at t <= 0.6 the position models omit almost everything and
reconstruct at ~5 dB vs ~12 dB for bit_only. The objective is file size at acceptable distortion,
so from this stage on decisions use rate at 20-35 dB, with the envelope reported alongside.
Re-checked the data-scaling decision with the same metric: n01024 still wins (see
`results/data_scaling/DECISION.md`).

Reproducibility: `bit_only` here is config-identical to `data_scaling/n01024`; its training
history and all 2,800 sweep rows (bytes, MSE, payload bits) are identical to that run.

Diagnostics only: val BCE 0.4846 / 0.4792 / 0.4654; the position models also train far more
stably (bit_only val BCE swings 0.485-0.570 at constant LR; bit_plane_rowcol 0.465-0.488).
